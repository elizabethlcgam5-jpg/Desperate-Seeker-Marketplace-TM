<h2>How Desperately Seeking Works</h2>
<p>Desperately Seeking is built to save you time. Instead of scrolling through endless listings, you just post what you need — and sellers come to you. It’s simple, fast, and made for people who don’t want to waste their day searching.</p>

<h3>1. Buyers Post What They Need</h3>
<p>Tell the community exactly what you're looking for. Be specific so sellers know if they have it.</p>

<h3>2. Sellers Respond With Matches</h3>
<p>Sellers check your post and reply if they have the item. No more digging through old listings — the right items come straight to you.</p>

<h3>3. Chat, Ask Questions, and Confirm</h3>
<p>Buyers and sellers can message each other to confirm details, ask questions, or negotiate before buying.</p>

<h3>4. Pay Inside the App</h3>
<p>Once you're ready, the buyer pays through the app. This keeps everything safe and makes sure the sale is recorded.</p>

<h3>5. Pick Up or Get It Shipped</h3>
<p>You can meet locally, do a porch pickup, or have the seller ship the item. Whatever works best.</p>

<h3>6. Sale Completed</h3>
<p>After payment, the seller gets their payout and the buyer gets their item. Easy, clean, and no confusion.</p>

<h2>Seller Rules & Expectations on Desperately Seeking</h2>
<p>Desperately Seeking is built to make selling simple. These quick guidelines help keep things smooth, safe, and fair for everyone using the app.</p>

<h3>1. Only Respond If You Really Have the Item</h3>
<p>Buyers post exactly what they need. Please only reply if you actually have the item and it matches what they’re asking for.</p>

<h3>2. Be Honest About Condition</h3>
<p>List the real condition — new, like new, good, fair, or needs work. No surprises when the buyer shows up.</p>

<h3>3. Communicate Clearly</h3>
<p>Answer questions, send photos if needed, and keep things simple. Good communication makes the sale faster.</p>

<h3>4. Use In-App Payments</h3>
<p>All sales should be completed inside the app. This protects both sides and makes sure the sale is recorded properly.</p>

<h3>5. Be On Time for Meet-Ups</h3>
<p>If you're doing a local pickup or meet-up, be reliable. Let the buyer know if you're running late or need to reschedule.</p>

<h3>6. No Spam or Fake Listings</h3>
<p>Only post real items you actually have. No scams, no fake posts, no misleading photos.</p>

<h3>7. Respect the Buyer</h3>
<p>Everyone’s here to save time. Be polite, be fair, and keep things simple.</p>

<h2>Local Pickup Safety Tips on Desperately Seeking</h2>
<p>Most people on Desperately Seeking are just regular buyers and sellers trying to save time. Still, it’s always smart to stay safe when meeting someone in person. Here are a few simple tips to keep things smooth and stress-free.</p>

<h3>1. Meet in a Public Place</h3>
<p>Choose a well-lit, busy location like a store parking lot, coffee shop, or police station safe zone. Avoid private homes unless you're doing a porch pickup.</p>

<h3>2. Bring Someone With You</h3>
<p>If possible, take a friend or family member along. Extra eyes always help.</p>

<h3>3. Keep Communication Inside the App</h3>
<p>Use the in-app chat so everything stays documented. Avoid giving out your phone number unless you’re comfortable.</p>

<h3>4. Inspect the Item Before You Confirm</h3>
<p>Check the item in person before marking the sale as completed. Make sure it matches the description and condition.</p>

<h3>5. Don’t Carry Large Amounts of Cash</h3>
<p>Payments should be done inside the app. No cash needed, no awkward exchanges.</p>

<h3>6. Trust Your Gut</h3>
<p>If something feels off, cancel the meetup. Your safety comes first, always.</p>

<h3>7. Porch Pickup? Keep It Simple</h3>
<p>For porch pickups, leave the item in a visible spot and confirm payment through the app. No need for face-to-face if you don’t want it.</p>
